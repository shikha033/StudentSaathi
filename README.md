# StudentSaathi-A-One-Stop-Digital-Guide-for-Student-Empowerment

##  Project Overview
Student Saathi is a web-based platform designed to assist students by providing structured guidance on essential services such as ID documents, certificates, government schemes, internships, and emergency helplines.

The platform simplifies complex procedures by presenting step-by-step information, required documents, official links, and resources in one place.

---

## Objectives
- Provide a centralized platform for student-related information  
- Simplify processes for documents like Aadhaar, PAN, etc.  
- Increase awareness about government schemes and opportunities   
- Offer emergency help and helpline information  

---

## Features

### ID & Document Help
- Aadhaar Card  
- PAN Card  
- Voter ID  
- Driving License  

### Certification Help Desk
- Income Certificate  
- Caste Certificate  
- Domicile Certificate  
- Bonafide Certificate  

### Government Schemes
- Central & State Schemes  
- SC/ST & Girls-focused Schemes  

### Skills & Internship Support
- NPTEL, SWAYAM Courses  
- Internship Platforms (Internshala, etc.)

### APAAR ID & Academic Results
- APAAR ID Information  
- Board Results (CBSE, State Boards)

### Emergency Help & Helplines
- Women Safety  
- Mental Health Support  
- Cybercrime Reporting  
- Anti-Ragging Help
